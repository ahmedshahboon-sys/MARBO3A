MARBO3A OFFICIAL BRAND ASSET POLICY

Canonical mark: /brand/official/marbo3a-mark.svg
The canonical mark is the approved MARBO3A identity rendered as a pure vector SVG with no embedded raster/base64 payload.
Use it for application chrome, login/onboarding, room cards, favicon/SVG fallbacks, OpenGraph artwork, offline/maintenance surfaces and browser caches.
PWA raster icons are committed derivatives of the same approved identity: /pwa-192.png, /pwa-512.png, /pwa-maskable-512.png and /apple-touch-icon.png.
Do not introduce independent logo artwork in components or public assets without updating this canonical source.
